Filtering Pipeline Architecture

The ArcadeVerse search and filter engine operates on a clean, single-pass pipeline directly over the established GAMES_REGISTRY data structure without duplicating datasets or modifying individual games:

1

Source Dataset

Consumes GAMES_REGISTRY compiled from individual game.json files across the repository.

2

Title & Keyword Search

Case-insensitive substring match on trimmed queries across title, description, and author.

3

Category Match

Filters by dynamically discovered categories or personal local bookmarks (Favorites).

4

Tag Criteria

Applies tag criteria with configurable selection semantics (OR match vs AND match).
