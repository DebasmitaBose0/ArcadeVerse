    HOW TO PLAY

    A busy little bakery

    Match every item on the customer's ticket, then serve the tray before patience runs out.

    • Choose: Click or tap a pastry, or press keys 1-6. Choose it again to add another.
    • Serve: Match the ticket exactly and press Enter or the serve button. Click a tray item to remove one.
    • Score: Fill orders for points. Fast service and an unbroken streak earn a bonus.
    • Survive: The shift timer is shared by every order. Three misses end the shift; each level brings larger orders and less patience.
    • Pause / restart: Press P or Escape to pause. Press R or use New shift to start over.
