Castaway Survival Guide

The Objective:

You crashed onto an uncharted tropical island. Manage your four vitals (Health, Hunger, Thirst, Energy), gather resources, construct shelters, and find a way to escape back to civilization.

Controls:

  • Movement: WASD / Arrow Keys or Touch D-pad.
  • Action: Spacebar or [ACT] button (chops trees, mines rocks, picks berries, drinks water, hunts wildlife).
  • Crafting: [C] key or [Craft] button.
  • Consume: [E] key or [Eat] button.
  • Rest / Sleep: [R] key or [Rest] button near a campfire or shelter.

Escape Routes:

1. The Seaworthy Raft: Craft a raft and launch from the shore.
2. The SOS Radio Beacon: Salvage the plane's battery and erect an antenna on the high cliff!
